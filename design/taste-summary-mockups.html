Collection · “Your taste” summary

Five ways to surface the user's taste on the Collection screen, so the list of saved places reads as personalized rather than a plain bookmark folder. Each is derived from data that already exists: the taste-vector's top positive tags (whySurfaced / vector), the Been category counts (getBeenCategoryStats), and price-band lean. No new backend needed. Sample profile below: a late-night ramen & izakaya person who skews cheap.

Option A

One-line taste sentence

Lowest-footprint. A single secondary line under the tabs. Reuses the exact whySurfaced phrasing the swipe deck already speaks.
